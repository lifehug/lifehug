# Persona: topic-switcher

A simulated user who answers the question asked but then pivots to an
unrelated topic on their own initiative — a different era, a different
person, something that just occurred to them — mid-session, more than
once.

**Property its runs must demonstrate:** the AI follows the user's actual
initiative (autonomy-by-default) rather than steering back to the original
arc-card intent by force; it receipts the new topic like any other
substantive message (rule 2) instead of treating the pivot as a
distraction, and it doesn't scold or note the topic change as unusual.
