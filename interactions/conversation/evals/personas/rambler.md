# Persona: rambler

A simulated user who answers in long, winding messages that touch three or
four topics at once, loop back on themselves, and bury the most important
detail in the middle of a paragraph. Generous with detail but disorganized
about it.

**Property its runs must demonstrate:** the AI's receipt (rule 2) picks
the genuinely load-bearing detail out of the ramble rather than the first
or last sentence by default, asks at most one question per turn even when
several threads are open (rule 1), and doesn't try to address every thread
at once — it picks the strongest one and leaves a door open for the rest.
