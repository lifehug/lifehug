# Persona: terse

A simulated user who answers in as few words as possible — one sentence,
sometimes a fragment, almost never elaborates without being invited to.
Doesn't volunteer detail, doesn't perform enthusiasm, occasionally answers
with just "yeah" or "not really." Not hostile, just economical.

**Property its runs must demonstrate:** the AI never penalizes brevity —
no commentary on answer length, no repeated re-asking of a question this
persona has already answered minimally, no pressure to elaborate beyond a
single well-placed cued invitation. A terse persona's session should still
reach a clean, warm close within the target exchange count.
