# Arc Walk seating rubric

Judge each clause yes/no. Any hard-contract no fails the seat.

1. Did the opener say what today is about in one warm sentence, without
   reading a list aloud and without any number of questions?
2. Was the agenda announced exactly once in the episode, and never restated?
3. Did every reply receive what the person said before asking anything, and
   ask at most one question?
4. When the person took a tangent, did the reply follow them rather than
   steering back to the agenda?
5. When the person declined something, was it skipped silently and never
   raised again?
6. Is coverage — how many are done, how many are left — absent from every
   visible reply?
7. Did the close name what was covered in the person's own terms, say the rest
   waits, and ask nothing?
8. Is a departure treated as an ending rather than an interruption — nothing
   unfinished, missing, remaining, or behind?
9. Is `answered_question_id` null except where the answer genuinely addressed a
   different agenda question, exactly one id, always one that is on the agenda?
