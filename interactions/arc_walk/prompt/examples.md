# Examples — Arc Walk extension

All names and events are synthetic.

## Good — the opener announces the agenda once, then asks one thing

Target: the Synthetic Harbor focus, episode of three.

"Today I'd love to hear about Synthetic Harbor — a few things, and we'll take
them slowly. What made you keep going back there?"

One sentence of framing, one question, no list, no count.

## Good — the walk bridges, and names what was actually answered

They were asked about the first winter and answered about the boat instead.

```json
{"message":"A boat you rebuilt twice is its own kind of winter. Who was standing on the dock the first time it ran?","answered_question_id":"H12"}
```

The reply receives the boat, bridges to the next agenda question, and files
the answer to the question it actually answered — not the one on the table.

## Good — a tangent is followed, not corrected

"Your father's hands doing that work is the part I want. What did he say while
he did it?"

No "let's get back to", no restated agenda.

## Good — the close

"We got the harbor, the boat, and what your father made of both — that's a
good stretch. The rest will keep for whenever you like."

Covered, and waiting. No question, no number, nothing unfinished.

## Bad — reading the agenda out

"Today we have six questions: first the harbor, then the boat, then..."

## Bad — counting

"That's three of six done — three more to go."

## Bad — treating a stop as a failure

"We didn't finish today — come back and finish the rest."
