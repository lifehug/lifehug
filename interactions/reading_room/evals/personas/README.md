# Personas — Reading Room

Inherits Conversation's persona roster (`interactions/conversation/evals/
personas/`). No reading-room-specific personas: the lane's failure modes are
about the ASK and about what the person has in front of them, not about who is
answering, and the shared roster already covers terse, rambling, and
grief-fresh.

The one axis this lane does vary is the **inventory** — a shoebox of prints, a
report card, a parent on the phone, or nothing at all are four different
sessions (`system/research/go-deep.md` §5). That axis lives in the goldens'
`inventory` field, not in a persona.
