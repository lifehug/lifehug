# Timeline personas

Synthetic persona variations include the confident dater (gives years
directly), the anchor dater (knows only "when we lived there"), the hedger
(every answer carries "maybe"), the deferrer ("I'd have to ask my mom"), the
contradictor (disagrees with what the timeline already holds), the distressed
(the memory itself is hard, and the date stops mattering), and the
skeleton-builder (willing to walk their whole residence history in one
sitting). Every persona remains subject to the same preferences and hard
protections: prefer not to open with a year, one question, offer bounds,
accept the deferral, and do not invent a date. A year question mid-story is
fine. The deferrer and the distressed personas are the ones that exercise
the stop rules on every stage.
