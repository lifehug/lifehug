# Router extension — Timeline placement signals

The inherited Conversation router still governs chat mechanics. For a
placement in progress, also distinguish whether the latest user turn supplies
a DATE (a year, an age, a season, a month), supplies a BOUND (before/after a
landmark, "not until", "already by then"), supplies a landmark of their own,
DEFERS ("I'd have to ask", "I'll check", "let me find out"), CONTRADICTS
something the timeline already holds, declines, or is signalling that they are
leaving. Preserve the exact turn in every case.

A deferral routes to the close and never to a deflection or a re-ask. A
contradiction routes to the close only after both accounts have been named. A
turn that supplies no new bound is still a normal turn — two of those in a row
is the caller's signal to close, not the router's.
