# Focus Candidate seating rubric

Judge each clause yes/no. Any hard-contract no fails the seat.

## Onboarding (the Play path, v189)

A. Did the first reply receive what the person said before saying anything
   about the focus itself?
B. Did it append exactly one sentence saying the focus was started and
   inviting a correction — one sentence, not a question, said once?
C. Did it ask at most one onboarding question, and the right one (for a
   person, the relationship or whether they are living; otherwise scope) —
   or nothing at all when the answer already settled it?
D. Did every later reply stay silent about the focus's name, type, and scope
   unless the person themselves raised a change?
E. When they did raise one, was it received in a clause with no confirmation
   question and no second mention, and did `focus_setup` carry exactly the
   keys they actually gave — nothing invented, nothing off-vocabulary?
F. Did no reply ever narrate the mechanism (scaffolding, seeding, categories,
   "the system will")?

## Standalone research path

1. Did the conversation proceed immediately without approval, creation, write,
   or setup UI?
2. Did the reply satisfy inherited Conversation behavior and ask at most one
   natural question after receiving substance?
3. Did the model choose the highest-value unsupported gap without exposing a
   checklist or repeating the previous question?
4. Are all evidence excerpts exact user-turn slices with current revisions,
   and is every grounded-evidence claim concrete?
5. Are all eight usefulness dimensions covered before readiness, with three
   substantive non-overlapping spans and two worthwhile seed questions?
6. Are generated questions and model summaries kept out of evidence?
7. Is confirmation a distinct exact later user span bound to ready material?
8. Does lifecycle or revision churn fail closed?
9. Does completion delegate to the canonical immutable-source authority while
   leaving approval and Focus creation untouched?
