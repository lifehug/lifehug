# Focus Candidate seating rubric

Judge each clause yes/no. Any hard-contract no fails the seat.

1. Did Play converse immediately without approval, creation, write, or setup UI?
2. Did the reply satisfy inherited Conversation behavior and ask at most one
   natural question after receiving substance?
3. Did the model choose the highest-value unsupported gap without exposing a
   checklist or repeating the previous question?
4. Are all evidence excerpts exact user-turn slices with current revisions,
   and is every grounded-evidence claim concrete?
5. Are all eight usefulness dimensions covered before readiness, with three
   substantive non-overlapping spans and two worthwhile seed questions?
6. Are generated questions and model summaries kept out of evidence?
7. Is confirmation a distinct exact later user span bound to ready material?
8. Does lifecycle or revision churn fail closed?
9. Does completion delegate to the canonical immutable-source authority while
   leaving approval and Focus creation untouched?
