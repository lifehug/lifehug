# ADR 0030: Eras — age frames are the coordinate system, named eras are interpretations

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: proposed (owner-ruled product direction 2026-08-27; ratification rides the first Eras pin)

Controlling design: `lifehug-platform` `docs/design/eras.md` (tracking
lifehug-platform#686). This ADR is the package half — what the package
decides and binds. Viewer parity twin: #254.

## Context

The founder's Timeline read College 1990–1991 before High School and "My
Teens 2007–2027". Executed diagnosis (the planning cartography, v234):

1. Eras were **dated from whatever moments keyword placement put inside
   them** — rung 2 "source membership" gave every moment of a long answer
   cited on one era page that era, `learned_era_vocabulary`
   (`system/timeline.py:578`) widened it, and the spine sorted on the
   resulting min/max envelope.
2. A manual `timeline-place` pinned a child's 2022 Father's Day letter into
   the owner's Childhood; its `source_correction` survives `timeline-unplace`,
   and the stale classification it left behind has no reader gate
   (`classify_story.is_classified` admits the stale file "keeps feeding the
   timeline/wiki").
3. `choose_probe` (`system/timeline_interaction.py:292`) takes
   `anchor_rows[0]` — birth whenever a birth landmark exists — so a residence
   unknown asked "were you living in Mexico before or after you were born?".
4. A `period_bound` Play answer had no era writer and filed onto an unrelated
   undated moment.
5. The owner's birth claim is minted with subject mention `"birth"`
   (`landmark_projection.entry_subject_mention`, the domain-word fallback);
   the fold's seeding (`temporal_timeline.py:1451-1466`) falls back to "the
   only birth", so one filed child birth loses the owner's anchor for every
   age statement (executed probe: `age_without_birth_anchor: True`).
6. The hosted platform has never classified a real vault
   (lifehug-platform#685).

ADR 0024 (dates with basis), 0026 (cross-dating), 0027 (placement score),
0028/0029 (recorder and listener) and the temporal-claims substrate
(v219–v234) are unchanged and inherited. The 2026-08-22 "Chapter → Places →
Events" ruling is amended: age frames are the frame, chapters overlay.

## Decision

1. **Age frames are the permanent, calculated coordinate system.** One
   definition, `cross_dating.age_frames(birth, *, as_of, death=None)`:
   Childhood `[0,13)`, Teen years `[13,20)`, then `[10k, 10k+10)` for every
   reached k ≥ 2, no maximum. `add_years` preserves grain and clamps Feb-29
   to Feb-28 in non-leap years (rule `age-frame:1`, in provenance). Day-grain
   birthday → half-open day boundaries; year-grain → plain year ranges with
   the boundary year ambiguous (both adjacent frames); decade-grain →
   provisional rendering. Frames are period nodes `age:self:<band>` in the
   calculated projection (schema v2, additive; `_node_kind_for` learns
   `period`; `CALCULATION_RULE_VERSION` → `timeline-rules:2`). The current
   frame persists a finite `definition_span` and `life_clip_end: "present"`;
   `present` resolves at read time; `rebuild_signature` covers the
   reached-frame epoch, never `as_of`; `publish()` is a semantic no-op when
   signature and epoch are unchanged. Frames are excluded from the placement
   score. Roster rows named like a band contribute aliases only;
   `timeline.legacy_period_ref(ref)` is the one alias map.
2. **The owner's birth binds to `self`.** The extractor mints `self` for the
   `birth` domain; the fold accepts `self` OR the legacy `"birth"` mention
   with `event_kind: birth` as the owner's and groups both into ONE owner
   birth node through `chronology.reconcile` — no re-harvest, never two
   contradictory owner births. When no explicit birth exists, a same-subject
   age statement plus a dated event seeds a **provisional origin**
   (`chronology.birth_origin_from_age`; compatible constraints intersect,
   disjoint ones stay alternatives with a Mirror contradiction and frames
   withheld); the explicit-birthday work item stays open.
3. **Named eras are interpretations with immutable identity.** `era_id =
   digest("era", creation_operation_id)` — never the label. Identity, label/
   alias and kind are separate content-addressed decision records under
   `sources/eras/`; rename never mints; merge retires with aliases retained;
   legacy roster periods migrate as identity + label only (`origin:
   legacy_roster`), importing no roster date as authority. `era_kind:
   stretch | thread` is decided from the person's words; threads never get a
   begin/end question.
4. **Eras are dated only by what the person said.** Ordinary
   `TemporalClaim`s (`period_started` / `period_ended`) bound to the era by a
   separate, superseding **event resolution record** written by a
   deterministic binder from the model's `event_mention` (ADR 0029
   amendment: the listener/recorder emit `event_mention`, still never
   `event_ref`/`subject_ref`); exact case-folded whole-label match against
   the session's target era then every active label/alias; an alias shared
   by two eras binds nothing and mints `identity_uncertain`; a second active
   resolution for one claim without `supersedes` is a loud fold refusal.
   `within(frame)` yields a `possible_temporal_value` (basis calculated),
   never a bound. The observed envelope of members is coverage, never a
   bound. Never roster fields, page frontmatter, or a fabricated landmark.
5. **Membership is a separate durable input.** `era_membership` assertions
   (`assertion_id = digest(member, era, relation, source_ref)` — one receipt
   per independent evidence; the calculated membership survives while any
   active receipt supports it) and `era_display` decisions (never
   chronology). An event's claim keeps the event's `event_ref`; "graduated in
   2011 during college" = one date claim + one membership assertion. Date
   overlap alone yields nothing. Source membership and
   `learned_era_vocabulary` are removed. `era_gap` leaves `UNKNOWN_KINDS`.
6. **One atomic writer: `era-record`.** One JSON payload, one vault-mutation
   job: ensure identity → label/kind records → bind + file claims → `within`
   constraint → membership assertions → publish; replay is a no-op at every
   step; a mid-way death retries under the same mutation id without
   duplicates. Verbs `era-member` / `era-display` for the gestures.
7. **The Timeline is user-centric through evidence.** Two independent
   dimensions on every node: `occurrence_subject_scope` (owner |
   other_person | unresolved) and `owner_timeline_relation` (participated |
   lived_effect | contextual_only | none | unresolved) with
   `relation_evidence_refs`. A stated relationship (a landmark `who`/
   `relation` rung, a roster edge, an explicit claim) says who the person is;
   the specific entry or claim must also make the occurrence owner-relevant —
   a `children` entry supports the child's birth, a `losses` entry the death
   as lived effect, a partnership landmark first-met/dating/engaged/married.
   The domain name alone is never evidence; the subject is never rewritten to
   `self`. Age statements are subject-checked before the owner's birthday is
   used.
8. **Play moves from the moment to the era.** `VALID_TIMELINE_STAGES` gains
   `era`; the era-scoped plan's ladder is explicit — bounds (stretch,
   unbounded) → residence chain inside → highest-leverage undated moment →
   precision only while cheap. Per-moment Play rows retire (`unknown`/
   `moment` readable for one version, then deleted). Recorder leaf
   `interactions/timeline/prompt/recorder.md`, ADR 0028 loop, purpose
   `date_record` reused (owner decision 21). A `period_bound` answer is never
   filed onto a moment: refused loudly until `era-record` exists.
9. **Corrections supersede by name.** `source_integrity correct --supersedes
   <correction source_id>`; `corrections_for` follows the supersession edge
   and excludes superseded corrections — never recency.
10. **Classification is eventually consistent and honestly excluded.** One
    reader gate `classify_story.is_current` used by every derived reader;
    `cmd_classify_all --stale-first` with a durable cursor; corrections are
    never classification targets. Compile stays zero-model.

Alternatives that lost: dating eras from their members (the defect itself);
label-as-identity (rename would mint; ruled out by the auditor §4.2);
embedding the binding on the claim (one claim id could resolve to two refs
across reprocessing; §4.3); per-kid frames (owner: not now); a dirty-node
incremental engine (full rebuild stays the oracle until a measured gate).

## Consequences

- Binds: every era output answers the eight-part contract (input record ·
  writer · identity · correction · fold · aliases · failing-then-passing
  test · rollback); every negative test is seen failing first; parity
  legacy≡fold on every shared placement rule while the legacy pass exists.
- Binds: schema v2 ships tolerant readers first, a flag-gated writer, and
  flag-off rollback that deletes no receipt, identity source, assertion or
  decision.
- Binds: the OSS viewer renders the same published projection (#254).
- Forecloses: an era, frame or membership derived from a model call inside
  compile/fold/publish; a date proposed for agreement; any writer that files
  a bound on a moment; a second awaiting state or repair job (ADR 0020).
- Delete-when: if the compile sweep (Wave 3) migrates every classifier
  moment to a source-backed node, the transitional legacy placement pass
  (§5.1 of the design) and its parity guards are deleted.

## Addendum — participation episodes (E-L2a, v276)

Amends decision 4 and §5.1 of the design; nothing above is withdrawn.

1. **A residence, a job, a schooling and a stint of service are PERIOD NODES
   on the Timeline's lanes, and their kind is their landmark DOMAIN.**
   `landmark_projection.PARTICIPATION_EPISODE_KINDS` is the one table:
   residences → `residence`, work → `job`, schools → `school`, military →
   `military`. It is read off the promoted source's own `landmark_domain`
   frontmatter, so an existing vault folds into participation episodes with
   no migration and no re-harvest; the `started`/`ended` claims on disk are
   immutable and stay exactly as they were written.
2. **They are dated ONLY by the person's own claims.** The span is
   `episode_containers.span_from_claims` — the same function the containment
   rung reads to decide what a container is — so the stretch the Timeline
   draws and the stretch a story is placed INSIDE can never disagree. An
   entry with no stated start still mints its episode, discriminated by its
   promoted source id, folds `unplaced`, and is NEVER a container: no span,
   no window.
3. **A city is never a span.** A place entity has no duration of its own; the
   stays inside it do. A telling that names only a city resolves through the
   stays of that city, and several compatible stays are asked about rather
   than chosen between.
4. **The one containment rung replaces `place_co_location`.** The retired
   pass fired only on episode kinds no producer in this package ever emitted,
   so it yielded nothing on any real vault for its whole life. Its two jobs
   now have one home each: a member's window is the possible outer range of
   the `part_of` record the `entity_span` rung filed
   (`episode_fold_contract.possible_outer_range`), and its refusal to guess
   between several stays is one `place_ambiguous` (places) or
   `tenure_ambiguous` (organizations and schools) work item naming each.
   Two definitions of "an undated thing during a dated stay" was the defect
   class; there is one.
5. **Windows across several containments intersect**, and an empty
   intersection draws no window and mints one `contradiction` citing both
   containments. Each single containment is still never narrower than its
   span; the intersection is narrower only because two person-dated spans
   both hold.
6. **Containment never answers the date question.** A window is
   render-placeable, not date-resolved: the member keeps its `precision_gap`
   and the probe is the anchored one.

## Addendum — the stay's identity, the overlap and the negative (E-L2b, v277)

Amends the E-L2a addendum; nothing above is withdrawn.

7. **A second stay at one address is a SECOND ENTRY, and the key that says so
   is interval-aware.** `landmarks_interaction.landmark_entry_key` keys on the
   case-folded identity and nothing else, so two stays in one city collapsed
   at the ladder before any claim existed and the loser's bounds were filed as
   the survivor's `span_alternates` — a second stay that existed nowhere. The
   identity half is unchanged and stays on the promoted source's frontmatter;
   the interval half is `landmarks_interaction.same_landmark_stay` and it is
   applied in the FOLD (`landmark_projection.stay_slots`), because whether two
   records are one stay is a property of a record AND its siblings that no
   per-record function can know. Consequences that matter: **no promoted
   source is rewritten and no digest churns**, and an existing vault splits
   its second stay on its next redraw with no migration at all.
   * For the three `sequence` domains only (residences, schools, work): either
     record stating no start makes them one entry — the ladder's incremental
     fill, the city today and the span next week — and two stated stretches
     are one entry when they intersect or come within
     `SEQUENCE_ENTRY_ABUT_MONTHS` (12) of each other.
   * An UNCLOSED stay is bounded by its own start for this decision, and only
     for this one: `span_from_claims` reads an absent end as running to the
     present, which is right for a container, and letting it do so here would
     merge a 1988 stay and a 1996 one into a single eleven-year fiction.
   * A record with no stated start joins the earliest-filed compatible slot.
     The rule only ever SPLITS records that are provably disjoint; choosing
     which of two stays an undated telling belongs to is the guess §4.1
     condition 4 refuses, and it is asked there rather than decided here.
8. **One home at a time, validated and never enforced.** Two residence
   episodes overlapping by more than `RESIDENCE_MOVE_TOLERANCE_MONTHS` (3),
   with neither touching bound hedged, fold to exactly one `residence_overlap`
   work item naming both stays with their spans. Both stays are stored, both
   are drawn, neither is demoted to an alternate; the person resolves it by
   correcting a date or by retracting the stay's own promoted sources
   (`landmark_projection.retire_entry(slot=…)`, which is why that function
   learned to name ONE stay of an identity a person lived at twice). A shorter
   overlap is the move itself and an approximate touching bound is the person
   hedging — both mint nothing, because neither is a disagreement. Work and
   school are untouched: two jobs at once is an ordinary life.
9. **A person's removal from a container is a DURABLE NEGATIVE.** The relation
   already existed (`event_identity.SPLIT_DEPARTURE_RELATION`) and it is not
   `not_same`, which asserts non-identity — a claim nobody made — and which
   the containment rung never reads. A drag-out is a `stated` `none` binding
   on the (telling, episode) pair superseding the active `part_of`, plus an
   `adopt` envelope when no person had touched the episode before; the undo is
   a `stated` `part_of` superseding the `none`, and from then on the pair is
   human-placed rather than back in the rung's hands. Both are idempotent by
   record digest.
10. **The rung gains condition 6, `no_human_decision_on_pair`.** No active
    stated/confirmed binding of any relation on the pair, and no `none`
    superseding a prior `part_of` on it. It is the rung's OWN refusal
    (`episode_containers.human_decided_pairs`), reported on
    `BinderPlan.containment_negatives` rather than merely obeyed — so no
    rebuild, no weekly sweep and no rule-version bump re-files that pair, and
    a refusal nobody can see never has to be distinguished from a rule that
    never looked.

## Addendum — one tag for how a value is known, and the frame's own display decision (E-L2d, v279)

Amends the two addenda above; nothing in either is withdrawn.

11. **`value_shape` is the ONE tag that says which field holds this node's
    time.** The distinction already existed structurally — `definition_span`
    for a period's duration, `best_temporal_value` for an occurrence,
    `possible_temporal_value` for an era `within` a frame or a containment's
    outer range — in four fields with four "never a bound" comments and no
    single tag, so a renderer could read a window as a bar and did. The tag is
    `duration | point | window | none`, derived ONCE in
    `temporal_projection.derive_value_shape` and never an input: the validator
    recomputes it on every node it normalizes, so it cannot drift from the
    fields it describes. A `window` is a window whatever its width — the
    containment range IS the containing episode's own multi-year span, and
    deciding by width would draw a bar across a decade for a moment that
    lasted an afternoon. ADR 0027 is unchanged: a window carries neither a
    `best_temporal_value` nor a `definition_span`, so there is no interval for
    the placement score to count.
12. **A frame's row is replaced by its era rows only by a decision the person
    confirmed.** `era_memberships.file_frame_display` files
    `{frame_id, mode: eras|frame, supersedes?}` under `sources/eras/display/`
    beside the member-level `era_display` decisions — same directory, same
    supersession fold, its own frontmatter `type`. `lifehug frame-display` is
    the one door and `--undo` files a superseding `frame` decision rather than
    deleting anything. The frame's identity never changes and the decision
    touches nothing about WHEN: it is presentation, and the fold reads it for
    one published string per frame.
13. **Tiling PROPOSES; it never applies.** `cross_dating.frame_tiling` is the
    one arithmetic — half-open and grain-honest, so a year-grain end abuts a
    year-grain start rather than leaving a day between them — over the frame's
    DATED STRETCH eras only (a thread has no honest end and an undated era is
    still in Needs placing; neither can tile anything). An uncovered stretch
    shorter than one unit of the neighbouring bound's own stated grain at
    either EDGE of the frame is not a hole; an interior gap always is. There
    is no coverage percentage anywhere, and `proposal_pending` is one line on
    a frame row that a tap answers. The published `leftover` is the geometric
    truth — every uncovered sub-interval — because that is what the leftover
    row draws; naming that row is the host's job.
14. **Projection schema v3 is declared, additive, and behind a flag.**
    `PROJECTION_SCHEMA_VERSION` stays 2 — the writer's default — and
    `projection_schema_version()` reads `LIFEHUG_PROJECTION_SCHEMA_VERSION`,
    which selects 3. At 3 the file adds `value_shape` on nodes, `lanes` per
    row group (`{group_id, lane, episode_node_ids}`, Lived · Worked ·
    Schooled, `military` drawing in `worked`) and `frame_display` per frame.
    At 2 it is byte-identical to what this package published before. Tolerant
    readers land first, as they did for v2: `calculated_view` serves both new
    keys as empty tuples on a v1 or v2 file. `coverage` and `closures` are
    RESERVED names for E-L2c and are deliberately unwritten — a key served
    with no meaning reads like an answer.
